# Governance decisions

## Working decisions implemented

- Scope is all York-developed AI solutions; MARS is one supported path.
- The official name is **AIDE Committee**, sponsored by the Office of the CIO. No acronym expansion is used.
- AIDE is advisory and provides documented recommendations. It does not approve, authorize, reject, stop, fund or deploy.
- AIDE and IPPM can refer work to each other, and proponents can approach IPPM directly.
- Conditional specialist reviews replace ITAC in the standard flow.
- Change Management review is the working production gate and must confirm an accepted named operational owner and support model; it does not provide ongoing support.
- Prototype boundaries are interim guidance pending approval. No timelines or service levels are represented.

## York confirmation required

- Authoritative institutional inventory platform
- Approved prototype guardrails
- Accountable Change Management production decision role
- Whether Change Management selects or verifies operational ownership
- Approved AIDE intake and review timelines
- Future APIs or submission integrations
- Whether the AIDE acronym has an approved expanded name
